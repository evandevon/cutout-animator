How it works

  1. Add a drawing. Photograph it on white paper and the paper is cut away. Draw around it or use the brushes to tidy it up.
  2. Set the scene. Choose a backdrop and sounds, then place your cutouts. Park cutouts in the dark area outside the frame so they can walk on later. That area isn't in the video.
  3. Press Record. Tell the story and drag your cutouts at the same time. Several people can drag different cutouts on a touch screen.
  4. Add to recording. Plays the scene back so you can move another cutout or add more voice. This is how to move several cutouts with a mouse.
  5. Make video joins your scenes into a video. Save project keeps a file you can open later and keep editing.

Moving cutouts

Drag to move. Pinch with two fingers, or use the round handle or mouse wheel, to resize and turn. Hold Shift with the wheel to turn. Double-tap to flip.

Keyboard

Tab chooses a cutout. Arrows move it, [ ] resize, , . turn, F flips, R records, Esc stops.
